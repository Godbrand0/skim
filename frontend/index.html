    How it works

    1. You send on Mezo. The funds go straight to the Skim treasury.
    2. When your transfer confirms, USDC is paid to your Base address from a pre-funded float, minus the fee.
    3. The treasury settles with Mezo later in larger batches, which is how small amounts become possible.

    Beta. The contracts have not been audited, and per-transfer and daily limits apply. If liquidity runs out, your transfer waits until the float is refilled.
